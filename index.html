REVIEW / AVerification summary only

Explanations still pass
through the platform.

Both parties can see that the two dates differ. The platform summary states that the correction preceded handover and that the gateway accepted a notification request.

However, the parties cannot independently check the versions, times and full notification behind the summary. Further questions or corrections still require the platform to select, relay and explain the materials.

The parties also need an opportunity to check the basis of the summary.

Supporting records and a route to a response
REVIEW / BThe same record is open to both parties

One suspicion can be revised;
further questions become more specific.

The disclosed platform record provides a checkable basis for Alex’s statement that the correction preceded handover; A’s summary already made the same claim. The difference between the pages alone no longer supports saying it was “changed after collection”.

The same log shows that the notification omitted the old and new dates; the gateway receipt confirms only acceptance of the request, not device delivery, opening, understanding or renewed consent.

Both parties gain an opportunity to check directly, while also taking on reading records and interpreting fields. The platform still needs to respond and correct errors.

Readable records, explanations and corrections
Unconfirmed in both conditions

The complete temperature history, actual device delivery, opening, understanding, renewed consent and the cause of the illness. Uncollected data establish neither success nor failure. Food should not be eaten after its use-by date, but being past that date alone does not establish the cause of this illness.

The same unknowns, different opportunities to check. A already provides the platform’s account; B lets the parties directly check its supporting record and challenge specific fields. The unknowns remain the same because B only discloses existing records, adds no monitoring and retains the same prior events.
